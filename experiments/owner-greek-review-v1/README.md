# Owner review v1: Greek review, meaning sources and authored pairs

The owner is a native Greek speaker and has offered to review. This is the first **human** review input the project has had. It unblocks three things that agents cannot provide.

| Part | What | Unblocks |
|---|---|---|
| A | The meaning, in your own words, of the 14 sources left unresolved in the meaning scorer (8 English, 6 Greek) | 14 of the 30 round-2 targets that cannot currently be scored (`meaning-human-review-packet-v1`) |
| B | The naturalness of the 19 AI-written Greek sentences (10 from round 2, 9 from V8) | #685-style language review for Greek. Any sentence marked unnatural gets a superseding versioned artifact; the frozen sets are never edited. |
| C | 40 English/Greek pairs that you write, from your own work | The cross-language convergence test ([ADR 0025](../../decisions/0025-contract-freeze-pending-held-out-evidence.md); the review's step 5): authored by someone other than the extracting model, and held out from every earlier change |

[`WORKSHEET.md`](WORKSHEET.md) is generated by [`build-worksheet.mjs`](build-worksheet.mjs) from the frozen files. It shows source text only: no model output, no Sem and no expected outcome, so the review is not anchored to what the model did.

**Time:** Part A takes about 30–45 minutes, Part B about 15 minutes, and Part C about an hour. The parts are independent, and A + B can be returned first.

## What happens with the answers

- **A:**
  - A session turns each answer into a reviewed target.
  - Your meaning is the reference. The mapping to Lunum-Sem is a separate technical step, labelled as agent work.
  - The v17 and original runs are then re-scored over 30 targets instead of 16.
- **B:**
  - An unnatural sentence is replaced in a new versioned set, never by editing the frozen one.
  - Any score that used it is marked.
- **C:**
  - The pairs are committed **before** any model sees them, as a pre-registration with the success bar (≥70% shared identity) and the kill bar (<40% → drop the EN/EL convergence claim).
  - Then two live runs are made with the frozen contract 0.19. The second run also measures run-to-run stability.

## Labelling

The returned file records:
- the reviewer's name, role and language competence;
- the date;
- for each item, its source hash.

It is human review by the project owner. It is independent of the extracting model, but not of the project.
